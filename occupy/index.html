Occupy OpenAI

Since July 22, we've been outside the offices of OpenAI in San Francisco, asking Sam Altman to call for an AI Treaty.

At first, we protested daily in front of Anthropic and Google DeepMind, asking their CEOs to take the first step and urge for an AI Treaty. But after the recent news of an OpenAI model committing multiple felonies, we moved on to Occupy OpenAI.

Over 1,000 employees of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind — including OpenAI's Chief Scientist — recently urged the US government to support international tools to pace frontier AI development. But unless governments actually enforce a slowdown, there is no guarantee these companies will stop.

So we'll keep coming back to 550 Terry A Francois Blvd, every day, until Sam Altman calls for an AI Treaty.
