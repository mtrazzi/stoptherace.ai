Outside the Bay

Start your own

  1. Start your group
    • Get some friends together and have a first meeting
      • Discuss AI issues. Pick a video to watch or essay to read to guide the discussion.
      • Watch an AI video together and discuss: The AI Doc (Netflix), or various YouTube videos (we can recommend some)
    • Hold regular meetups (~weekly) and work on recruiting people to join you
      • Social media event pages
      • Members invite people from their social networks
      • Tabling/flyering in busy places

  2. Build a coalition Optional
    • Find groups your members are connected to and ask if they will sign onto our mission (an international treaty governing frontier AI) and be listed on your website/receive updates

  3. Make a website, or have us help you
    • List photos from actions
    • List coalition partners
    • Blog if you’d like
    • Post about upcoming events

  4. Plan an action for November 14th
    • Decide what it will be: rally, march, etc.
    • Work out details
      • Check early if permits are necessary
      • Where will you be? Try to pick somewhere symbolic to international cooperation.
      • Who will speak? Do you need amplification?
      • Various fun things (music, snacks, piñatas, etc.)
    • Promote the event
    • Hold a meetup to make signs/banners

  5. Make relationships with your Congressional representatives
    • Host a party where everyone calls your reps
    • Request a meeting with your Senators’ and House reps’ AI staffer
      • Prep help is available for this

  6. Banner drops
    • Make a banner and display it over a busy overpass (check local laws for legality)
