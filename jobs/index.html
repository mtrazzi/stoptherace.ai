Organizing & Operations Lead
$100k/year · San Francisco or New York · Full-time, in person

What you'd own

The machine that makes our actions happen: permits, police liaison, routes, venues, volunteers, contractors, money. If a march goes off cleanly, that's you. Reports directly to the founder.

Responsibilities

  • Logistics for marches, protests and shoots: permits, police liaison, routes, venues, staging, sound, food, supplies
  • Volunteer coordination and pipeline management for a growing base
  • Scheduling and project management across protest cycles, roughly one major action every two months
  • Coalition coordination with partners: Evitable, QuitGPT, PauseAI, StopAI, plus labor and civic groups (SAG-AFTRA, California Nurses Association, SF Labor Council, SEIU 1021, Alphabet Workers Union-CWA, Tech Workers Coalition)
  • Sustained protest operations at frontier AI companies
  • Press support: releases, media lists, journalist outreach. You're the ops backbone, not the lead press person.
  • Hiring and managing contractors: videographers, designers, event support
  • Finance ops: cards, receipts, monthly reconciliation with our accountant, grant reporting

How we work

  • Work on what moves the current goal forward
  • Disagree once, then commit to the decision
  • Own what goes wrong, including what you didn't see coming
  • Propose concrete experiments instead of debating in the abstract
  • Report what you tried, with numbers
  • Manage up: flag what's slipping before anyone asks
  • Bring decisions as a yes/no with a recommendation
  • Write down outcomes so anyone can pick up where you left off

You're a fit if

  • You've run logistics for a 100 to 1,000 person event: permits, police liaison, staging, volunteer roles, contingency plans. Sunrise, 350.org, campaign field, union organizing all fit.
  • You run a project management system without being asked, hold 30 threads without dropping them, and send the follow-up before anyone asks
  • You can talk to a union rep, a nurse, a campaign staffer and an AI safety researcher in the same week
  • You believe frontier AI development is dangerous and that an international AI Treaty matters
  • You understand messaging discipline: credentialed speakers, no fringe rhetoric on stage, no property damage
  • Bonus: labor, healthcare union, or bipartisan coalition experience

You're probably not a fit if

  • You're a comms or PR person without operations experience
  • You've never run a live event with permits, police liaison and 200+ attendees
  • Your only organizing experience is online
  • You need close management. This role extends the founder; it isn't a junior position.
  • You can't publicly associate your name with the movement

Full-time, W-2, $100k/year. Fixed term through January 31, 2027, renewable subject to funding. Start as soon as possible. In person in San Francisco or New York City. US work authorization required; no visa sponsorship.
